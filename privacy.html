Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026 · App: Quadrop (Android, package org.ajjk.quadrop)

Quadrop is a four-in-a-row game with an AI buddy that learns as you play. It's made for everyone, including children. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. The short version: your games, your buddy and your Chips stay on your device. The only information that leaves it goes to our ad provider (Google AdMob) and our crash reporter (Sentry), and, if you buy something, to Google Play, as described below.

Information stored only on your device

  • Your birth year, asked once when you first open the app. We use it only to choose the right kind of ads (see Children). It never leaves your device.
  • Game data: match history, your buddy (its name, character, level and what it has learned), your Chips balance, items you've unlocked and your chosen skins.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this from the device. If Android backup is turned on, Android may also keep a copy in your own Google account's backup so it can be restored on a new phone; you control that in Android settings (Settings → System → Backup), and we can't see it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: rewarded ads you choose to watch (for Chips or to unlock a character), ads between games, and an ad when you return to the app. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the device model, operating system, IP address, and, for players 13 and older, the advertising ID. How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before any personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

If the birth year entered means the player may be under 13, the app tells Google AdMob to treat every ad request as directed to a child: no personalized ads, no advertising ID, only G-rated (general audience) ads, and no ads when returning to the app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no chat, no public profiles and no way to share personal information with other players.

Parents or guardians with questions can contact us at the address below.

Purchases (Google Play)

You can buy premium currency, an ad-free upgrade, the everything bundle and some skin sets with real money. Every purchase is made through Google Play: Google handles the payment and your payment details, and we never see them. Google gives the app a purchase record (which product, when, and a purchase token) so it can unlock what you bought; the app keeps it on your device. If the birth year entered means the player may be under 13, the app asks a parental check question before every real-money purchase, and Google Play's own parental controls can also require a parent's approval. See the Google Payments privacy notice.

If you reinstall the app or move to a new phone, "Restore purchases" in the Store gets back anything you bought for good (premium currency you had already received is stored on the device and isn't restored).

Crash reports (Sentry)

If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report to Sentry so we can fix the problem. A report contains technical information only: what went wrong in the code, the app version, the device model, the Android version and similar details. It does not include your name, your advertising ID, screenshots or anything you type. We have configured Sentry not to store IP addresses. See Sentry's privacy policy.

Online features

The current version of the app works entirely offline. If a future version adds online features (for example syncing your buddy or playing other people), it will create an anonymous guest account identified by a random ID and the display name you choose, and store your games on our server. We will update this policy before that happens.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't ask for your name, email, contacts, location or photos. (You can type a name for your buddy; it stays on your device. Kids should pick a fun name rather than their own.)
  • We don't see or store your payment details; Google Play handles every payment.
  • Chips are earned by playing and can't be bought with real money.

Keeping and deleting data

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. Crash reports are kept by Sentry for up to 90 days. To ask about or request deletion of any information, contact us below.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
